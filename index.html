Privacy Policy

TIXLY RUSH · Developed by Zibidi Studio
Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TIXLY RUSH, a mobile puzzle game developed by Zibidi Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our application.

2. Information Collection

TIXLY RUSH does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

However, third-party services integrated into the application may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers and device information.
  • Advertisement interactions and app usage information.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

TIXLY RUSH uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as IP addresses, advertising identifiers, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, personalization where permitted, and fraud prevention.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Notifications

TIXLY RUSH uses Unity Mobile Notifications to schedule local notifications on your device. These may include daily reward reminders and reminders to return to the game.

Notification permissions are controlled through your device's operating system. You can enable or disable notifications through your device settings.

5. Local Storage

Game progress, preferences, and certain game settings may be stored locally on your device to maintain your gameplay experience.

Locally stored information can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

6. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob: Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies: Privacy Policy

7. Data Security

We do not operate a player account system or a dedicated server database for storing players' personal information.

Information collected by third-party services is handled according to their respective security practices and privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

TIXLY RUSH does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may process device or advertising information subject to their applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you believe that information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You can:

  • Enable or disable notifications through device settings.
  • Manage or reset your advertising identifier through Android settings.
  • Uninstall the application to stop using it and remove locally stored game data.

You can also review Google's advertising controls at Google My Ad Center .

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services, or applicable legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.
